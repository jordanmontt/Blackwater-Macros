A state-of-the-art target that learns from you

Every calorie app starts with a formula, and formulas describe the average person. For you, the formula can be off by hundreds of calories a day. That's enough to lose weight when you meant to maintain, without knowing why.

To measure what you burn, the app compares what you ate with how your weight moved. Every Monday it combines the two numbers, and the more precise one counts more. With little data the formula leads. The more you log, the more your own numbers count.

Every number explained

Every number in the app says where it comes from. Tap it and you see the formula with your own numbers filled in, the studies behind it, and what it can't tell you.

Calories
Your basal rate, times what you really do (gym days, session length, walking), not a "lightly active" guess. Never below your basal rate.
Protein
A range from sports-nutrition research for your goal. It's counted on your lean mass when you log body fat. Eating more than the range is fine.

Questions

Should I install from F-Droid or from this page?

Either one. F-Droid builds the app from the source code and updates it for you. The APK on this page is built by GitHub from the same code, and the app tells you when a new version is out. They are signed with different keys, so to switch from one to the other you uninstall first. Sync your account or export your data before you do.

How do I update the APK?

When a new version is out, the app shows a notice on the Meals screen. Tap Download, open the file, and Android installs it over the old one. Your data stays.

How do I know the download is genuine?

Every release on GitHub comes with a SHA-256 checksum next to the APK. The app's signing certificate has this SHA-256 fingerprint:

Do I need an account?

Not on Android: the app works fully without one. The web version needs an account, and accounts are by invitation for now. With an account, the phone and the web version stay in sync.
